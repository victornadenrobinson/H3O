## 2. Physics: open questions

**P1. Insertion vs crystal mixing are consistent, and the dilute limit is strongly negative.** Insertion gives the dilute chemical potential (mu_H - G_H ~ -0.5 eV, about -1 kT, after the pressure correction). The crystal value is the average over 27 added H (x = 0.25 H/O, e.g. +76 meV at 500 GPa 6000 K, +141 at 5500 K). A linear (regular-solution) fit through the corrected n = 1, 2, 4 averages extrapolates to +55 (5500 K), -83 (6000 K) and +275 (400 GPa 5000 K) at n = 27, against +141, +76 and +192 direct: agreement within ~100-150 meV, which is the noise of a 7x extrapolation. So mu_H rises steeply with concentration (~20 meV per added H in 108 O at constant P). The fixed-V pressure rise explains only ~25% of the raw insertion slope.
- Consequence: H is soluble in superionic fcc H2O up to roughly **x ~ 0.07-0.15 H/O** (differential mu_H = G_H(pure)) at 400-500 GPa, 5000-6000 K. A positive per-added-H average at x = 0.25 does NOT mean "insoluble" (summarize label fixed; the report generator, not in this repo, still says "positive = H not soluble").
- Needed: points at x ~ 0.05-0.15 to locate the solubility limit (insertion n = 8, 16, or crystal runs `hper` 2.074 / 2.111 / 2.148 with 108 O; the empty `fcc_400GPa_6500K_h2.0xxxxo` dirs were meant for this), and long runs (insertion n=1 has +-30-45 meV and 30-85 meV drift at 2 ps).

**P1b. Temperature dependence of dilute insertion is NOT resolved (2026-10-27).** From the report's n -> 0 values (300/5000 -325, 400/5000 -400, 400/6500 -305, 500/5500 -497, 500/6500 -548, 500/7000 -403 meV): ds = -0.57 +- 0.24 k_B with all six (chi2/dof 7.6/3); -0.36 +- 0.31 without 500/7000; -0.38 +- 0.28 without 400/6500; at 500 GPa 5500 -> 6500 K dg goes DOWN by 51 +- 43 meV. The earlier -0.97 k_B came from insertion_model.py, which picked the longest pure-H run regardless of dt (BUG, fixed: now same dt as the insertion runs, like summarize.pick). dg/dP = -1.1 +- 0.2 meV/GPa is robust. Solubility vs T needs the n = 8, 16 runs (115897), not the dilute ds. Reference bookkeeping checked analytically for U = 0: mu - G/N = kT[ln((N+1)/N) - ln(2 pi N)/(2N)] = -5 meV at N 250, 6000 K: no T-dependent error there.

**P1c. Electronic entropy (2026-10-27, not yet run on data).** Training labels are TOTEN (electronic free energy at each AIMD run's SIGMA). Pure H at 400-500 GPa is a metal: free-electron estimate -T S_el ~ -(pi^2/6) g(E_F)(kT)^2 ~ -30 / -43 / -60 meV/atom at 5000 / 6000 / 7000 K (r_s ~ 1.3, E_F ~ 15 eV); superionic H2O has a gap. So electronic free energy may be a sizeable, T-dependent part of mu_H - G_H (favouring pure H more at high T), and it is only right if SIGMA = kT of each run. `tdi/elec_entropy.py <frames.extxyz>` gets -T S_el = 2 (TOTEN - E_sigma0) per frame and reports (1) the T scaling of pure H (SIGMA = kT or fixed?) and (2) the electronic part of mu_H - G_H per T bin (synthetic test: exact).
RESULT on valid_m + train_m (932 frames, 300-650 GPa): SIGMA followed kT (pure-H -T S_el / (T^2 scaling) = 1.00-1.09 from 3000 to 9000 K; -29 / -59 meV/atom at 5000 / 7000 K). Water metallises too (H2O -5 / -46 / -143 meV/atom at 5000 / 7000 / 9000 K). Electronic part of mu_H - G_H: -0.9 (3000 K), -29.7 (5000), -29.1 (7000), +35.8 (9000) meV per H: small and flat over 5000-7000 K, so not the source of the T dependence. **Ideal-solution reference (page 8 of the report, paper text).** Identical mobile protons, no -kT ln x singularity: mu_id = kT ln(N_H) + const, N_H = (2 + a) N_O, so d mu_id / da = kT / (2 + a) (differential), kT/4 for the per-added-H AVERAGE that the insertion and crystal rows are. Lattice-gas variant with M sites per O: S_id = -k N_O [c' ln(c'/M) + (M - c') ln(1 - c'/M)], c' = 2 + x, gives a differential slope kT M / (c' (M - c')) = kT M / (2 (M - 2)) at x -> 0 (M = 3: 3 kT / 2; M -> inf: kT / 2). The TDI assumes no lattice (all configurational entropy is in the integral); M only changes the guide line. Measured slopes (18-52 meV per added H in 108 O = 2-5.6 eV per unit H/O) are far above any ideal value: the concentration dependence is non-ideal (plus the uncorrected cell pressure rise). State the finite mu_H(x -> 0) explicitly in the paper.

## 4. Known limitations, not fixed (judgement calls)
- `17_eos_md.build()`: H on random bond midpoints: exact composition but locally 0-4 H per O, H-H down to d_OO/2 in the start frame.
- Restarted nodes reseed the RNG from the seed (not a bias, not a bit-exact continuation).
- 5-block errors: +-35% relative uncertainty; +-1 SEM covers ~63% (4 dof). Errors of short (<= 2 ps) runs are optimistic; use the drift column.
- Finite size: ln N! Stirling terms (~ -6 meV per added H at 6000 K for 108 O / 250 H); 96-atom liquid cells; only one H finite-size pair (400 GPa 6500 K, N 250 vs 500: equal within 5 meV/atom).
- Toy LJ blows up at the smallest lambda (bare r^-12): toy-only, but any potential with a stiff core needs dt checks at lambda < 1e-6.
- The report generator is `mlp_pipeline/tdi/report.py` (branch claude/eloquent-ramanujan-2tjx2p, merged here): figures + tables from summarize.py CSVs; check its labels and fits against this list.
