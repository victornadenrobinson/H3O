## Scientific objective

We are investigating hydrogen insertion thermodynamics in superionic FCC H₂O at 200–600 GPa and 2000–6000 K using a fine-tuned GRACE machine-learning potential.

Our calculation uses proxy-guided insertion/deletion, Rosenbluth reweighting, and Bennett acceptance ratio (BAR) to calculate hydrogen chemical potentials and insertion free energies.

The automated pipeline should support arbitrary P–T conditions, four additional hydrogen counts (`--n 5 11 16 22`), multiple temperatures, automatic EOS generation, and safe resumption of existing calculations.

We recently developed new scripts, which are now committed but not yet thoroughly validated.

## Task 1 — Audit EOS generation

Inspect `build_eos_2000.py` and its inventory dependencies.

Verify:

- Multiple pressures and temperatures are supported.
- EOS fitting and root selection are mathematically and physically sensible.
- Bulk modulus is correctly calculated and positive.
- Temperature interpolation works.
- Unrestricted pressure/temperature extrapolation is actually implemented.
- Extrapolated results are explicitly marked and warned about.
- `--force` is required to overwrite existing JSON files.
- Automatically generated EOS JSONs contain the correct fields and compositions.

**Important inventory issue:** `runs/inventory_binned.csv` has approximately 10,025 data rows, whereas an untracked `mlp_pipeline/inventory_binned.csv` on Perlmutter has approximately 64,497 rows containing unrelated iron–wüstite and silicate calculations.

The larger inventory was deliberately NOT committed. Determine the correct intended inventory, ensure composition filtering excludes unrelated materials, and make the inventory path configurable where appropriate.

Do not manufacture replacement DFT data.

## Task 3 — Validate the scientific estimator

The current method uses:

- K=2000 proxy candidates per selected host snapshot.
- 8 Boltzmann-selected insertion candidates.
- 4 reverse deletion trials per full-system snapshot.
- Batched GRACE single-point energies.
- Rosenbluth-corrected forward and reverse work.
- BAR and forward/reverse exponential estimators.

Review the mathematical correctness of the selection probabilities, correction terms, work sign conventions and BAR normalization.

Saved work arrays use:

- `forward_work_eV`
- `reverse_work_eV`

The stored reverse work is already sign-adjusted for the production BAR call.

Verify whether the implementation produces unbiased estimates under the intended ensemble and assumptions, including unequal numbers of insertion and deletion samples.

Check the ideal chemical-potential and pressure corrections.

Do not rewrite the scientific estimator without a demonstrable error and suitable regression tests.

## Task 4 — Validate and improve RDF diagnostics

Inspect `mlp_pipeline/plot_pwidom_diagnostics.py`.

A previously identified bug mixed coordinates from the independent host and full-system trajectories.

The extra-H calculation must use the N+1 configuration itself, e.g.:

`delta = full[frame, :-1] - full[frame, -1]`

Check that the fix is present.

Verify periodic boundary handling, species selection, cell reconstruction and finite-size normalization.

Existing trajectory caches contain `host`, `full`, `numbers`, `V`, `T`, and sometimes `cell`.

Do not assume a cubic cell unless confirmed or explicitly requested.

Ensure figures clearly distinguish:

- Equilibrium host RDFs.
- Local environments around a tagged hydrogen in the N+1 system.
- True insertion-conditioned, statistically weighted RDFs (which may require additional saved data).

## Task 5 — Implement BAR convergence diagnostics

Create `mlp_pipeline/plot_bar_convergence.py` if missing.

Read existing `*_work.npz` files and produce:

- Cumulative BAR chemical potential versus trajectory fraction.
- Forward and reverse work distributions.
- BAR overlap diagnostics.
- Kish effective-sample fraction.
- Contiguous block-to-block BAR estimates.
- CSV output with numerical diagnostics.

Use the production BAR estimator or independently verify equivalence.

Where corresponding result JSONs are available, check that the final BAR estimate reproduces `mu_ex_bar_meV`.

Make it explicit that sampling fraction is not physical time unless frame timing is available.

Do not treat correlated insertion trials as statistically independent when reporting uncertainty.
