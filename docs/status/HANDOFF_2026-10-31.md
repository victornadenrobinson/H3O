completed parent, holds an exclusive lock, backs up files under `_history/`,
relocates historical JSON/command paths, then writes combined samples/results
at the normal top-level paths. Old-proxy replays are archived and need rerunning
on the combined frames. Without `--in-place`, fresh output behavior remains.
