## Extension behavior now implemented

`--extend-run DIR --in-place` keeps the same working directory. It requires a
completed parent, holds an exclusive lock, backs up files under `_history/`,
relocates historical JSON/command paths, then writes combined samples/results
at the normal top-level paths. Old-proxy replays are archived and need rerunning
on the combined frames. Without `--in-place`, fresh output behavior remains.
Two successive in-place extensions, archived cache preservation and lock refusal
were exercised with the CPU toy model. Interrupted MD still has no periodic
checkpoint; recover from the completed parent archive, not partial MD memory.
