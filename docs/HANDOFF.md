## 6. Physics status and plan
- Phase-diagram scenarios (schematics computed by hull construction, NOT results): `docs/figures/phase_diagram_scenarios.png` and `phase_diagram_hulls.png` (`phase_diagram_examples.py`): (a) miscible fluid, liquidus falls from Tm with added H ("melting by dissolution": SI-H2O cannot exist if bulk H/O exceeds the liquidus composition), (b) congruent H3O + two eutectics, (c) incongruent (peritectic) H3O -> SI-H2O + H-rich fluid, (d) SI-H2O solid solution, (e) fluid miscibility gap / monotectic.
- `tdi/tx_diagram.py` (existing, reads `docs/data/liquid_mix_grace.csv` + `melt_dG_grace.csv`, both from the OLD `ft_m2` TDI): liquidus at 400 GPa from Tm(H2O) ~6600 K: 6000 K -> H3.6O, 5500 K -> H5.4O, 5000 K -> H9.2O; 500 GPa Tm ~7200 K, 6000 K -> H3.9O; solvus <= H2.11O. Not trustworthy: ft_m2 had pair c +411 meV/H, and the direct H2.25O check disagrees with the fit by 30+ meV/atom.
- Huang et al. PNAS 2020 (notes in `docs/LITERATURE_NOTES.md`): Cmca H3O (64-atom cell `docs/data/h3o_huang.cell`; our training supercells 384 atoms) stable vs H2O + H2 above ~450 GPa (0 K + ZPE); superionic at 1250 K, melts at ~5250 K at ~540 GPa (AIMD heating, likely superheated; no high-T free energies). Since the H2O liquidus at H/O = 3 (~6300 K at 500 GPa, old data) lies above H3O's melting point, H3O would melt incongruently: scenario (c) above ~450 GPa IF superionic H3O is on the hull at all; (a) below.
- **Gupta, Stixrude & Schlichting (arXiv:2407.04685, PBEsol)**: no EOS data, but their fitted coexistence model gives an H2-H2O critical curve (`docs/data/gupta2024_critical_curve.csv`): T_c ~5100 K (300 GPa), 5300 K (400), 5430 K (500), 5730 K (1000); x_c ~ H7O. Below T_c: two fluids. So at 5000-5500 K (where the liquidus and planets are) a miscibility gap for H-rich compositions is plausible: do NOT dismiss the 300 GPa two-liquid region of tx_diagram as noise until the final model has checked it. Cheap test: EOS / MD at 5000 K for H5O and H7O and look for demixing (a concentration-structure diagnostic in 17_eos_md is proposed, not written); proper test: liquid TDI dense in composition at 5000 K.
- **Physics queue after the final model**: (1) liquid TDI on a dense composition grid (H2O, H2.25, 2.5, 3, 3.5, 4, 5, 6 O + pure H 729) at 300-600 GPa (`perl_tdi_states.sh`); (2) SI-H2O melting (G_liq - G_solid) at the same P; (3) TDI / Frenkel-Ladd of superionic Cmca-H3O (384 atoms) at 500-600 GPa, 2000-5000 K -> `tx_diagram.py --compound 3:DG:DS`; (4) PWidom mu_H in the solids with the final model; (5) bands from the G errors; k-point correction already in the model.

- dG per added H includes the ideal-dilution term, pressure and finite-size corrections; single-H insertion is only meaningful in the atomic fluid (use `mol_frac`).
- Pressure convention: DFT/hull data and 17_eos_md P include the ionic N kB T / V term; stress labels exclude it.
- Thermostat history: friction 0.01 (~1 ps) vs 100 fs; frame caches store md_settings; do not mix old/new results.
- Gamma-only labels: k-point bias -53 meV/H in H-rich cells (section 3).
- GRACE short-range H-H hole: pairs pulled below ~0.1 A fall into a ~50 eV pocket (HHWALL option exists); the EOS flags report H-H < 0.25 A as COLLAPSE.
