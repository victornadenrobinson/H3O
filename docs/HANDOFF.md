## Key conventions and cautions
- dG per added H includes the ideal-dilution term, pressure and finite-size corrections; single-H insertion is only meaningful in the atomic fluid (use `mol_frac`).
- Thermostat bug history: friction 0.01 (~1 ps) vs 100 fs; frames caches store md_settings; mixing old/new results is a trap.
- Gamma-only labels are fine for insulating molecular cells with edges >= ~8 A; k-point bias ~ -53 meV/H in H-rich cells.
